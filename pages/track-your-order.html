Frequently Asked Questions

Where Is My Order? ⌄
Once your package has been shipped, we will send you an email that provides your tracking number. If you are unable to find this email, you can also track your package using the form above. Most orders will arrive within 7-10 business days.
When Will My Order Ship? ⌄
Orders placed Monday-Friday before 2pm ET are packaged and picked-up for shipping the same day. We also include 2-day shipping within the continental U.S.
My Order Status Hasn't Changed In Several Days. What Should I Do? ⌄
Occasionally, tracking information may not update right away. If your order status hasn't changed in 5+ business days, please contact us at hello@lookoptic.com or call (877) 857-LOOK and we will investigate.
What Is Your Return And Exchange Policy? ⌄
In the unlikely event that you do not LOOK great in your readers, you have 90 days from the date of purchase to return them, no questions asked. Exchanges are free. However, due to increased shipping and handling costs, we do charge a $8 fee for returned items.
Can I Edit My Order Or Update My Shipping Address? ⌄
To cancel or edit an order please email hello@lookoptic.com and include your order number within 1 hour of purchase. Please note, if the request is made more than one hour after purchase we may not be able to honor the request, as our warehouse is super fast!
